Why this workshop

Bring your nursing questions. Curiosity is all you need.

Large language models such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini are changing how people find, communicate, and evaluate health information. What do these tools do well—and where might they fall short?

Join nursing students, practicing nurses, nursing educators, and nurse researchers to test ideas, examine limitations, and discuss responsible AI use in nursing. Participants will work in small groups on nursing-focused scenarios, experiment with AI tools, and share insights.

No coding or prior AI experience required

Important: Workshop activities use hypothetical scenarios only. Do not enter real patient or other confidential information into AI tools. AI outputs are not a substitute for clinical judgment.
What to expect

Learn by doing, questioning, and collaborating.

Short, facilitated activities inspired by the nursing AI evaluation approach used at AI VITALS Check.

01 / EXPLORE

Try an LLM

Use a hypothetical nursing or healthcare scenario to explore how a model responds to questions and prompts.

02 / EVALUATE

Challenge the response

Identify missing context, potential bias, uncertainty, and safety concerns. Compare perspectives within your group.

03 / REFLECT

Share insights

Discuss where AI might support practice or education, where caution is needed, and what responsible use could look like.
